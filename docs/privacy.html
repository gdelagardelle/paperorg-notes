Privacy Policy

Paperorg Notes · Last updated: 22 August 2026

Paperorg Notes (“the App”) is a voice recording and transcription app for iOS, operated by Germain Delagardelle (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the App processes, where it is stored, and what choices you have. We designed the App with privacy and GDPR compliance in mind.

1. Summary

  • Audio and transcripts are stored on your device by default.
  • Free plan: includes 30 minutes of cloud transcription and AI summaries per month, without API keys or sign-in. Audio is processed through the Paperorg backend and its selected AI processors.
  • Paperorg Pro: transcription is processed via our secure backend; audio is sent to our server and then to AI processors — you do not need to supply API keys.
  • For cloud processing, we store a random device identifier and monthly usage minutes to enforce the applicable plan limit. Pro subscriptions are managed through Apple.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not use your content to train AI models unless a provider's terms state otherwise and you have consented.

3. Cloud Processing and Paperorg Pro

The included Free allowance (30 minutes per month) and Paperorg Pro use cloud processing. Audio for transcription and text for summarisation are sent to our backend over HTTPS. Our server holds the provider credentials and forwards the necessary request to enabled AI processors, such as OpenAI, ElevenLabs, or LuxASR, on your behalf. We record the transcription minutes used per month to enforce the plan limit. Pro currently includes 600 minutes per month.

  • The Free allowance is associated with a random device identifier generated on your iPhone; it is not an account and does not transfer between devices.
  • For Pro, we receive subscription events from Apple (purchase, renewal, cancellation).
  • The application does not create database records for audio, transcripts, or summaries; the originals remain on your device. AI processors may handle submitted content under their own terms.
  • Pro audio retention (90 days on device) is configurable in the App.

4. Third-Party Transcription Services

When you use included Free minutes or Paperorg Pro, you consent through the App's privacy screen to the cloud processing described in section 3. The App sends the required audio and/or transcript to Paperorg and the enabled AI processor to carry out your request.

5. Legal Basis (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area and Luxembourg:

  • Consent — cloud processing of the audio and text required for a transcription or summary request, after you accept this policy; separate consent applies when you enable a personal provider key.
  • Contract / legitimate use — local recording, storage, and features you request on your device.

6. Your Rights

You can exercise the following rights directly in the App (Settings → Privacy & GDPR):

  • Access — export all notes, transcripts, and audio (GDPR export).
  • Erasure — delete all data, including audio, transcripts, stored credentials, and any provider API keys saved by earlier versions of the App.
  • Rectification — edit transcripts; corrected text is stored separately from the original.
  • Portability — export as JSON, plain text, Markdown, or PDF.
  • Object / restrict — decline cloud processing; use on-device transcription where available.

You may also contact us at germain@delagardelle.lu to exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy.

7. Data Retention

  • Data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
  • You may enable “Delete audio after transcription” or automatic deletion after a set number of days.
  • Third-party AI processors may retain submitted data according to their own policies and applicable service terms.

8. Security

  • Provider credentials are held by the Paperorg backend and never enter the App. Credentials the App does hold, such as your Pro access token, are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Audio files use iOS Data Protection.
  • All network communication with transcription providers uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Optional Face ID lock protects access to the App.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
