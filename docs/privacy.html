Data protection

Privacy notice

Last updated: 31 August 2026

In short: recordings and notes live on your iPhone. They leave it only when you ask for a transcript or a summary, and then only to be turned into text and sent straight back. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

1. Who is responsible

Luxxow S.à r.l.-S, 12 rue Treeschheck, L-3321 Berchem, Luxembourg (R.C.S. Luxembourg B311306), is the controller for this app within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). Contact: hello@luxxow.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

Your recordings, transcripts, summaries and any notes you write are stored on the device. Deleting a note deletes its audio with it, and deleting the app removes the lot.

The microphone is used only while you are recording, and only after iOS has asked your permission.

3. What happens when you transcribe

Transcription and summarising do not run on the device. When you ask for either, the recording is sent to our own service at notes-api.paperorg.com, which passes it to the provider that does the work and returns the text to your iPhone.

The providers we use for that are:

  • LuxASR, operated by the University of Luxembourg, for Luxembourgish speech.
  • OpenAI, for speech in other languages and for summaries and action items.
  • ElevenLabs, for speech processing.

They act as processors on our instructions. OpenAI and ElevenLabs are established outside the European Economic Area, so a transcript made through them involves a transfer out of the EEA. LuxASR is in Luxembourg.

  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)) — it is the feature you asked for.
  • Nothing is sent while you only record and play back locally. The transfer happens at the moment you ask for a transcript or a summary.

4. Your account and Pro

The free tier includes a monthly allowance of cloud minutes. Paperorg Pro raises it. Accounts and entitlements are handled by Paperorg Platform, and a purchase is made through Apple, not by us: we are told that a subscription is valid, not how you paid for it. We never see your card.

5. Checking the app is genuine

Before the service will transcribe anything, it checks that the request came from a real, unmodified copy of the app, using Apple's App Attest. That check identifies the app, not you, and it exists so that the transcription allowance cannot be drained by something pretending to be Paperorg Notes.

6. What we do not do

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK and no third-party tracking in this app. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not use your recordings to train anything.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for access to your personal data, its rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to hello@luxxow.com and we will answer within one month.

If you believe we have handled your data badly you may lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg supervisory authority: Commission nationale pour la protection des données (CNPD), 15 boulevard du Jazz, L-4370 Belvaux — cnpd.public.lu.

8. Children

Paperorg Notes is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.
