# How to use Paperorg Notes

Paperorg Notes records what you said, turns it into a transcript and a short
note, and can email that by itself. You do not paste API keys.

## Record

1. Pick the language you will speak. Luxembourgish goes to LuxASR first.
2. Pick a note style (meeting notes, summary, raw transcript, …).
3. Tap the orange button, speak, tap Stop.
4. When it is ready, the note is on the Notes tab. If you added recipients in
   Settings, the email has already gone.

## Import a file

On the Record screen, choose **Import audio file** and pick an MP3, WAV or M4A
from Files (iOS) or from this phone (Android). It becomes a note the same way a
recording does.

## How long

| Plan | This month | One recording |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 30 minutes | 3 minutes — a voice memo, not a meeting |
| Pro | 10 hours | 3 hours |

The monthly allowance cannot say this on its own: one long file would spend the
month in a request you cannot undo. Free stops at three minutes so the 30
minutes are ten memos, not one dump.

If a recording hits the cap, the app stops and transcribes what you already
said. An import that is longer is refused before it is uploaded.

## Email

Add who should receive the note in Settings. Paperorg sends it when
transcription finishes. You do not set up Mail on the phone for that.

## Privacy

Audio can be deleted after transcription. Notes stay on the device. GDPR export
and delete-all are in Settings.
