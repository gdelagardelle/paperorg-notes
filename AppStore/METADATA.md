## Description

### English

**Stop writing meeting notes. Start sending them.**

Paperorg Notes closes every meeting for you — in the language you actually spoke.

Record a client call, team sync, or brainstorm. Within about 90 seconds you get:
• An accurate transcript (Lëtzebuergesch, German, French, English, Portuguese)
• A structured summary with decisions and action items
• A PDF and audio attachment — ready to email

**Built in Luxembourg, for Luxembourg**
Lëtzebuergesch is first-class — powered by LuxASR (University of Luxembourg), not an afterthought. Switch language mid-recording when your meeting changes language.

**Free — 3 meetings per month**
No account. No API keys. No setup. About 30 minutes of transcription each month, enough for three typical meetings.

**Paperorg Pro — Meeting Closure**
• 600 minutes/month — serious professional volume
• Auto-email summary + PDF after every note
• Unlimited custom vocabulary (client names, projects)
• 90-day audio retention
• Priority Lëtzebuergesch transcription pipeline

Your recordings stay on your iPhone. Audio is sent for transcription only when you ask. GDPR-aware. No ads. No tracking.

Download free. Record your next meeting. Leave the room already done.
